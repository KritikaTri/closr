A SOFTER KIND OF ONLINE

How closr works

Your home shows the status your person chose to share. Use the profile icon to set your status; it stays until you change it. Voice commands can include a duration.

My chat is private from your partner, but messages are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. Our space is visible to both of you. Only the shared notes in Our memories are included in either conversation.

A gentle ping sends no message. It appears as an in-app note the next time the other demo profile opens this browser. This is a local preview: profiles are samples and do not sync across devices.
