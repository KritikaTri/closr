closr

Your space. On your Home Screen.

Add Closr from your browser, then open it from its own icon. You can use the same account on iPhone and Android.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if that option appears, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the installation, then open Closr from its icon.

This is the browser-installed version of Closr. It needs internet to sync. Phone push notifications are not connected yet. Camera and microphone access is requested only when you choose to record.
